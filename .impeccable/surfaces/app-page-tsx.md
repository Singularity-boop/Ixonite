## Direction contract

THESIS: A pentest is evidence work. The page is a chain-of-custody file: every claim is an exhibit with a tag, a handler and a seal. Refuses the category default of a dark neon "hacker" hero with a shield icon and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Form-white paper ground printed with fine blue-grey form rules and boxed fields; black ink; evidence-marker yellow strictly for exhibit tags and the primary action; tamper-seal red strictly for seals and critical severity; kraft brown only for the evidence-bag report band. Faces: Big Shoulders Display (stencilled crate headings), Public Sans (form body), Courier Prime (typed custody entries and data). State is a mark, not a hue (raise from cutting-bench challenger): stamps, punched corners, seal tape. Primaries rationed by role (raise from Bauhaus challenger).

STORY: The visitor sees that Ixonite breaks in and hands back proof, reads a real-looking (synthetic, labelled) finding, trusts the handlers, then books or takes the sample report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width custody form sheet. Form header strip as nav. The DESCRIPTION field holds the headline at display scale. Right column of boxed fields: case no., scope, handler. The SIGNATURE / INTAKE field holds a working email input + "Book a scoping call" yellow button, and a "sample report" link beside it. A red tamper tape crosses the sheet's lower edge.
