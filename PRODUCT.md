> Written from the owner's brief (relayed voice note) without a live interview round. Items marked *(inferred)* need confirmation.

## Platform

web

## Users

- Founders, CTOs, engineering leads and security/compliance owners at software companies who need an independent penetration test, usually because a customer, auditor (SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS) or investor asked for one, or before a major launch. *(inferred)*
- Their job on the site: understand what Ixonite tests, judge whether the work is credible (the sample report is the main proof), and book a scoping call.

## Product Purpose

Ixonite (ixonit.com) is a cybersecurity services company. The site exists to turn a visitor into a booked scoping call or a captured email (via contact form or sample-report download).

## Positioning

Manual, human-led penetration testing with web application pentesting as the flagship service, delivered as a clear, developer-readable report. *(inferred; the owner will refine)*

## Operating Context

- Buyers compare vendors by reading sample reports, checking who the testers are (LinkedIn), and asking for a quote.
- Hosting: shared hosting. The site must be deployable as static files (Next.js static export); any form backend must work without a Node server (e.g. a PHP mail script on the host or a third-party form endpoint).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (provisional; final list to come from the owner): Web application penetration testing (main), Network penetration testing, Mobile/application penetration testing, SOC services.
- Contact email: contact@ixonit.com (placeholder).
- Features required: book appointment / contact form, sample report download CTAs in several places, team section with LinkedIn links.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ixonite. Domain: ixonit.com.
- Reference the owner named: aikido.dev, as a quality bar only. Do not copy it.

## Evidence on Hand

- None yet. No real logo, team photos, client logos, testimonials, certifications, or report. Placeholders must be clearly marked; do not invent customer names, stats, certifications or testimonials.
- Sample report: placeholder PDF at `public/sample-pentest-report.pdf` until the real one is supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work: the report and methodology are the proof, not adjectives.
2. Every page leads to one of two actions: book a call or get the sample report.
3. Plain language for non-security buyers, precise enough for engineers.
